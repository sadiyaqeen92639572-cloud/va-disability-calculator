FAQ

How much is 100% VA disability per month?

$3,938.58/mo for a veteran alone with no dependents, effective 2025-12-01. Add a spouse, children, or dependent parents and the amount increases — see the rate table above.

Do ratings below 30% get extra pay for dependents?

No. Ratings of 10% and 20% pay a flat rate ($180.42/mo and $356.66/mo respectively) with no dependent add-on. The dependent-status matrix only applies at 30% and above.
