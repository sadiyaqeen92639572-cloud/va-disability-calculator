# VA Disability Calculator Suite

Free calculators for combining VA disability ratings and estimating monthly pay, compensation with dependents, and back pay — based on VA's official whole-person combined-rating formula (38 CFR §4.25-4.26), not simple addition.

Live at **[combinedratingcalc.com](https://combinedratingcalc.com)**.

## What This Includes

- **Combined rating calculator** — repeater input for any number of disabilities, applies VA's whole-person formula with an optional bilateral-factor toggle (38 CFR §4.26) for paired-limb conditions
- **Monthly pay calculator** — looks up your actual dollar amount by rating percentage and dependent status from VA's published rate tables
- **Compensation calculator** — full dependent-status matrix (spouse, children, parents, aid & attendance)
- **Back pay calculator** — multi-period repeater that applies the historically correct COLA rate to each elapsed month, not a flat current-year rate

## Why Ratings Don't Just Add Up

VA rates the whole person. Each additional disability is combined against your *remaining* non-disabled percentage, not added directly — two 50% ratings combine to 75%, not 100%. Only the final result is rounded to the nearest 10.

## Compliance

VA claims representation is regulated (38 CFR §14.636) — fees on initial claims are restricted until after a decision is issued. The site routes users who haven't filed yet or are awaiting a decision to free accredited VSO resources (VFW, DAV, American Legion) instead of paid representation.

This is an independent tool — not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, not a VSO, and not a law firm.

## Build

Zero-dependency static HTML, generated by `generate-pages.js`. Rate tables and the combined-ratings table are sourced from official VA/38 CFR data with `source`/`last_verified` fields — the build blocks (`assertComplete()`) if those are missing.

```
node generate-pages.js   # build pages from data/
node generate-sitemap.js # rebuild sitemap.xml
node test-calc-engine.js # verify calc-engine.js against fixtures
```

Deployed on Cloudflare Pages, auto-builds on push to `main`.
