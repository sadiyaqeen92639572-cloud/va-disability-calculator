How this calculator works

Deterministic math — no AI, no estimation model. Official formula below.

Sort ratings descending.
combined = first rating
FOR each remaining rating r:
  combined = combined + (100 − combined) × (r ÷ 100)
Round combined to nearest 10 (38 CFR §4.25 Table I)

Bilateral factor (§4.26), if 2+ ratings affect paired limbs:
  bilateral_sub = combine(paired ratings)
  bilateral_sub += bilateral_sub × 10%
  combined = combine(bilateral_sub, remaining ratings)

This is the official VA "whole person" combination method, not simple addition — see how combined ratings work for a worked example.

FAQ

Why doesn't the VA just add up my disability percentages?

VA uses a "whole person" theory: each new disability is rated against how much of the veteran's remaining, undisabled efficiency is left — not against the original 100%. Two 50% ratings don't combine to 100%; they combine to 75%, rounded to 80% per the official Combined Ratings Table.

What is the VA bilateral factor?

If a veteran has two or more disabilities affecting paired limbs — both arms, both legs, or paired skeletal muscle groups (not one arm and one leg) — those disabilities are first combined together, then that sub-total is increased by an extra 10% before being combined with the veteran's other ratings.

Does this calculator cover Special Monthly Compensation (SMC)?

No. SMC applies to specific catastrophic disabilities (loss of use of a limb, need for aid and attendance, etc.) and is not calculated here — consult an accredited VSO if you believe SMC may apply to your case.
